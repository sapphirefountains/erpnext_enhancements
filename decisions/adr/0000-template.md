# NNNN. Short title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by `NNNN-….md`
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forced a decision — the constraint, the failure, or the tension between two things we
wanted. Enough that a reader who wasn't here understands the pressure. Not a description of
the solution.

If a claim is about the live system, say what it was verified against and when, the way the
work items do.

## Decision

What we chose, stated plainly and in the present tense.

## Consequences

What this costs as well as what it buys: what is now harder, which invariants a future
contributor has to preserve, and what would have to change before it is worth revisiting.
