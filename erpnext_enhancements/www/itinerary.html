{# Traveler itinerary shell (served at /itinerary; controller: itinerary.py). Chrome-free mobile page following the Time Kiosk shell pattern (kiosk.html). Its jobs: * declare the home-screen app (itinerary-manifest.json: "Sapphire Itinerary", scope /itinerary) and its touch icon; * load the itinerary styles (css/travel/itinerary.css) and front-end script (js/travel/itinerary.js); * hand the server-computed boot payload (employee, active trips — the ones they travel on and the ones they own — csrf) to the front-end as window.ITIN_BOOT / ITIN_CSRF / ITIN_BUILD. boot_json is escaped for an inline script by itinerary.py (script_json), which is what makes `| safe` below safe. The picture viewer is drawn straight into , over the root, so redrawing the page never closes it. The below is the first paint only: itinerary.js retitles the page for whose itinerary is shown. Leaflet maps are lazy-loaded from frappe's bundled assets on first use. The Contacts card at the top of the trip (911, the travel desk, who booked it, the trip lead, the job site, each hotel with urgent care nearby) and the "Print / save as PDF" link to the trip sheet are drawn there too, from the same answer; neither adds a history entry, and nothing here changes for them. Offline: itinerary.js registers /itinerary-sw.js?v=<ITIN_BUILD> with scope /itinerary (never the site root, which is the kiosk's). The worker keeps this page, its stylesheet and script, and the person's files; the page keeps each answer in IndexedDB. A page kept on the phone still carries the boot of whoever opened it, so before it shows anything saved itinerary.js checks the boot's offline_key against the ee_itinerary_key cookie (the offline marker, which itinerary.py sets and every sign-out deletes) and the boot's user against the session's user_id cookie. Cache busting: raw /assets are served with a 1-year immutable Cache-Control, so every mutable asset URL below carries ?v={{ deploy_version }} (see itinerary.py / kiosk.py::get_deploy_version). The worker's precache list is exactly the two ?v= assets below (tests/test_itinerary_service_worker.py).
