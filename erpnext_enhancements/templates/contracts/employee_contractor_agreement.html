3. CONFIDENTIALITY

3.1The Employee-Contractor recognizes and acknowledges that the marketing plans and business strategy, the names and addresses of the Company’s customers, the names and addresses of the Company’s suppliers, trade secrets and any other confidential and proprietary information concerning the business or affairs of the Company (including but not limited to marketing and business plans and strategies) (hereinafter collectively referred to as the “Confidential Information”) constitute a valuable, proprietary, special and unique asset of the Company’s business. The Employee-Contractor further recognizes and acknowledges that any communications, whether written, oral or otherwise, that the Company or any of the Company’s employees or contractors has with the Company’s existing or prospective customers and clients are extremely confidential (hereinafter the “Confidential Communications”). The term Confidential Information shall exclude any information that has been made public through no fault of the Employee or Contractor.

3.2The Employee or Contractor shall not, for any reason whatsoever, during or after the termination of his/her employment with the Company, use, disclose or allow access to, for his/her own benefit or for that of another, the Confidential Information or the Confidential Communications (or any part thereof) to any person, firm, corporation, association, or other entity for any reason or for any purpose whatsoever. {{ initial('confidentiality', 'Never using or disclosing the Company’s confidential information, during or after') }}

4. ASSIGNMENT OF RIGHTS

4.1Any and all information, data, inventions, discoveries, materials, notebooks and other work product which the Employee or Contractor conceives, develops or acquires during his/her employment with the Company, which directly or indirectly relates to work performed for the Company, shall be the sole and exclusive property of the Company. The Employee or Contractor shall promptly execute any and all documents necessary and take such further actions as the Company may deem necessary to assign any and all of the Employee’s or Contractor’s right, title and interest in such property to the Company. {{ initial('assignment', 'Work product belongs to the Company') }}

5. NON-COMPETITION

5.1For a period of 36 months from date of termination or contract completion from the Company, you will not directly or indirectly engage clients, contacts, and related parties for jobs done for the Company or solicit its employees. {{ initial('non_solicitation', 'No work with the Company’s clients, and no soliciting its employees, for 36 months') }}

5.2The Employee agrees that during his/her employment and 36 months past termination of employment, he/she shall not (without the express prior written consent of the CEO/Owner of the Company) directly or indirectly compete with the Company. In construing the foregoing prohibition, the Employee or Contractor shall be deemed to be competing with the Company if he/she shall become self-employed in, or accept employment with, consult with, render services to or become associated with, own, manage, operate, join, control, or participate in the ownership, management, operation, or control of, or be connected in any material manner with, directly or indirectly enter into the employment of, or make a substantial investment in, any corporation, partnership, proprietorship or other type of business organization or entity which engages in, any business involving any clients or customers that currently or have done business with the Company during the employee’s or contractor’s tenure with the Company. {{ initial('non_compete', 'No competing with the Company for 36 months after leaving') }}
